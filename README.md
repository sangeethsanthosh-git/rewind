## 📻 What is REWIND?

**REWIND** turns your modern smartphone into a photo-realistic, authentic vintage portable cassette player. Rather than presenting sterile flat lists, REWIND treats music with the dignity of physical media: heavy mechanical button clicks, an interactive 3D acrylic cassette jewel box opening, rotating spool hubs that move tape across the deck in real time, and an authentic Walkman chassis with a transparent acrylic inspection window.

Built from the ground up with **Flutter**, **Riverpod**, and **Just Audio**, REWIND is **100% offline-first**: no cloud logins, no telemetry, no ads, and zero battery-draining network requests.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📼 1. Authentic Walkman Chassis Mode & Custom Skins
- **Interactive Cassette Swapping**: Cycle through your collection by swiping horizontally on the cassette or tapping the tactile PREV / NEXT tape crate controls while the case is open.
- **Physical Cassette Insertion Sequence**: Choose your cassette, insert it into the player bay, close the hinged acrylic lid, and press physical play to listen.
- **Hinged Acrylic Inspection Door**: Watch your selected cassette tape rotate through a crystal-clear acrylic inspection window.
- **Custom Image Skins & Themes**: Wrap the Walkman chassis with your own custom image skin or choose from presets (**Classic TPS-L2**, **Solar Yellow**, **Stealth Black**, **Vintage Silver**, and **Tokyo Burgundy**).
- **One-Touch Eject & Swap**: Tap "SWAP TAPE" or press the physical OPEN / STOP keys to pop the door open, slide the cassette back into its J-card case, and choose another tape.
- **Dynamic Cassette Spools**: Real-time rotating hubs with realistic magnetic tape winding between supply and take-up reels.

### 🖼️ 2. Full-Bleed Cassette Face & Banner Art
- The track's album artwork spans the entire cassette face sticker with an authentic vintage gradient and centered tape transport inspection cutout.
- Custom banner support: assign any custom picture to local audio tracks.

### 🎚️ 3. 3D Dynamic Song Stack & Wallpaper Backdrop
- Signature acrylic crate of physical cassette cases on the home screen.
- **Crystal Transparent Backdrop**: The opaque intermediate shelf background has been eliminated so your custom wallpaper and blur canvas shine directly behind your cassette slabs.
- Scroll smoothly through your music crate and tap any track to play instantly with immediate audio response.

### 📜 4. Intelligent Conditional Lyrics
- Automatic extraction of embedded ID3 USLT / Vorbis lyrics and `.lrc` companion files.
- **Strict Privacy & Clean UI**: If a track contains lyrics in its metadata, an elegant retro-styled lyrics tab appears; if no lyrics exist in metadata, the interface stays completely uncluttered with zero placeholders.

### 🎨 5. Universal App Wallpaper & Blur Engine
- Upload your favorite wallpaper image as the full-app background.
- Adjust background blur in real time from **0 px (crystal crisp)** to **25 px (dreamy glass)**, with customizable dim levels.
- Applied seamlessly across Home, Library, Walkman, Folders, and Settings screens.

### 🔊 6. Synthesized Mechanical Sound Engine (Walkman Scoped)
- Authentic audio feedback recorded from vintage analog cassette hardware, strictly scoped to the Walkman chassis:
  - Solid spring-loaded **Play Clacks**
  - Crisp **Pause Clicks**
  - Metallic **Chassis Door Latches**
  - Heavy **Cassette Insertion & Ejection**
  - Smooth **Motor Hum & Wow-Flutter Ambience**

### 🎼 7. Lossless Audio Fidelity
- Native local playback for **FLAC (up to 24-bit/192kHz), WAV, MP3, AAC, ALAC, OGG, and Opus**.
- Gapless playback, automatic metadata tagging, and customizable playlists ("Mixtapes").

## 🛠️ Building from Source

```bash
# 1. Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/sangeethsanthosh-git/rewind.git
cd rewind

# 2. Get Flutter dependencies
flutter pub get

# 3. Run test suite
flutter test

# 4. Build release split APKs
flutter build apk --release --split-per-abi
```
